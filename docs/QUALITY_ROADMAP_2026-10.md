# Gridiron quality priorities — October 2026

This is a proposed sequence. Existing surfaces include a weekly game calendar,
game/team drilldowns, margin/total/push probabilities, scenarios, brackets,
season archives and a Forecast Lab.

Reference: [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/) separates schedules, scores,
teams, standings and injury news. Keep football's hierarchy and original branding.

1. **Keep evidence reproducible.** This pass verifies committed live metrics
   and horizons. Next, preserve repeated pre-kickoff snapshots and distinguish
   first from latest forecasts. Resolve historical price timestamps before
   calling them closing prices. Acceptance: recovery preserves cohorts and
   post-kickoff rows never qualify as live forecasts.
2. **Improve a measured weakness.** The Sep29 artifact has margin Brier .22002
   versus Elo .21990. Evaluate recency, quarterback/injury context and calibration
   with weekly rolling origins, train-only fitting, paired samples and a final
   held-out season. Report win quality separately from lattice margin/push and
   total quality, with intervals and forecast horizons. Retain Elo unless a
   challenger earns promotion; no improvement is asserted by this plan.
3. **Make week and playoff context immediate.** Preserve week/team filters
   across drilldown/back; lead with matchup/status/probability. Show injury age
   and forecast horizon, keep pushes distinct and explain using real features.
   Acceptance: mobile/keyboard flows browse a week, compare teams and return
   without losing context; errors and missing data remain readable.

Model changes follow temporal evidence; interface changes follow browser,
performance and accessibility QA.
